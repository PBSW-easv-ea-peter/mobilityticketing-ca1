# Mobility Ticketing System

## Introduction

You have been given a design brief for a mobility and ticketing platform. Your task is to iteratively design and build the system. You will be designing and building the system from a data-centric system-design perspective.

Week to week, you will be given a new feature or requirement. You will need to design and build the system to support the new feature or requirement.

Besides design and implementation, you'll do reviews of certain parts of the system. These reviews should focus on how well the system supports its intended workloads: route search, ticket purchase, ticket validation, timetable updates, real-time availability, and reporting.

## Short description of the scenario

The platform supports buses, trams, and trains in a city.

Customers can search for routes, view departures and delays, buy digital tickets, and validate tickets when boarding. Operators can maintain routes and timetables, update products and prices, and view usage and revenue reports.

The system is expected to handle rush-hour traffic, frequent real-time updates, correctness-critical purchases and validations, and long-term storage for reporting and compliance.

## Design brief

Parts of the design brief will be published with each week's lecture. This means that you don't know the whole design brief upfront. You will need to iteratively design and build the system based on the information provided in each week's lecture.
